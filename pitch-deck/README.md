# Pinnacle Designs Pitch Deck

PowerPoint pitch deck for client meetings, partnerships, and investor conversations.

## Files

| File | Format |
|------|--------|
| [pinnacle-designs-pitch-deck.pptx](./pinnacle-designs-pitch-deck.pptx) | PowerPoint (editable) |
| [pinnacle-designs-pitch-deck.pdf](./pinnacle-designs-pitch-deck.pdf) | PDF (share / print) |

11 slides, 16:9, brand colors (black + blue).

## Slides

1. **Title** — Logo, tagline, positioning
2. **The Problem** — Why local businesses struggle online
3. **Market Opportunity** — East Tennessee small-business focus
4. **Our Solution** — Website-as-a-Service model
5. **Why Pinnacle** — Traditional agencies vs. Pinnacle Designs
6. **Services & Pricing** — Base Camp, Ascent, Summit
7. **Client Outcomes** — Security, local SEO, peace of mind + guarantee
8. **Management Software** — Future vertical products (in development)
9. **Business Model** — Revenue streams and retention
10. **Traction & Readiness** — Site, contracts, analytics live
11. **The Ask** — Contact and next steps

## Regenerate

```bash
python scripts/generate-pitch-deck.py      # PowerPoint
python scripts/generate-pitch-deck-pdf.py  # PDF
```

## Presenting

- Export to PDF from PowerPoint for email attachments
- Use **Presenter View** for speaker notes (add your own in PowerPoint after opening)
- Customize slide 10 (Traction) with real client counts and revenue as you grow
