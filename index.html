FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Common questions from East Tennessee business owners about our web design and Website-as-a-Service plans.

How much does a website cost in Erwin, TN?

Plans start with a $500 down payment plus $99 per month for a Starter site. Growth and e-commerce plans are available for larger businesses. That monthly fee includes hosting, SSL security, and ongoing content updates.

What is Website-as-a-Service (WaaS)?

Instead of paying thousands upfront and managing the site yourself, you pay a small setup fee and a flat monthly subscription. We build your site, handle hosting and security, and make updates when you need them.

Do you work with businesses outside Erwin?

Yes. We serve Unicoi County and the greater Tri-Cities area, including Johnson City, Jonesborough, and Kingsport. We focus on local small businesses across East Tennessee.

What is included in the monthly subscription?

Hosting, SSL certificate, security monitoring, and a set number of content update hours each month. Menu changes, photo swaps, and text edits are included; larger projects like new pages are quoted separately.
