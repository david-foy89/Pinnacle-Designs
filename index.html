FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Common questions from small business owners about our web design, tools, and Website-as-a-Service plans.

Do you work with businesses outside Tennessee?

Yes. We serve small businesses nationwide. Design, hosting, and monthly updates are handled remotely, so your location does not change the plan or the price. Contact us for a free proposal — we respond within one business day.

How does remote web design work?

You share your business details, photos, and goals. We design the site, send proofs for approval, launch it, and keep it updated when you text or email changes. Every plan includes mobile-friendly design, hosting, SSL, and monthly content updates.

What makes Pinnacle Designs different from other web designers?

We combine affordable upfront pricing with ongoing Website-as-a-Service management. You get a custom site plus a dedicated partner who handles hosting, security, SEO basics, and text or photo updates — without the $3,000–$10,000 price tag of traditional agencies.

How much does a small business website cost?

Plans start with a $500 down payment plus $99 per month for our Base Camp plan, anywhere in the U.S. Ascent and Summit plans are available for larger businesses. That monthly fee includes hosting, SSL security, and ongoing content updates.

Are you building management software for my industry?

Yes. We are developing industry-specific management tools for home services, retail, restaurants, and professional services. Products are in active development — contact us with your business type to learn about early access.

Do you have live tools and calculators I can try?

Yes. Browse our Our Work section for live products including contract analysis, contractor and restaurant calculators, finance tools, Pinnacle Toolbox, and production sites like Pinnacle Weddings.

What is Website-as-a-Service (WaaS)?

Instead of paying thousands upfront and managing the site yourself, you pay a small setup fee and a flat monthly subscription. We build your site, handle hosting and security, and make updates when you need them.

Where is Pinnacle Designs based?

Our studio is in Erwin, Tennessee. The websites, tools, and software we build are for small businesses across the United States.

What is included in the monthly subscription?

Hosting, SSL certificate, security monitoring, and a set number of content update hours each month. Menu changes, photo swaps, and text edits are included; larger projects like new pages are quoted separately.
